# Primary sources — STOV conventions

## Chong et al. 2020 (ref_chong2020)

- A. Chong, C. Wan, J. Chen, Q. Zhan, "Generation of spatiotemporal optical
  vortices with controllable transverse orbital angular momentum",
  **Nature Photonics 14**, 350–354 (2020).
- DOI: 10.1038/s41566-020-0627-8
- Used for: the STOV (x, t) transverse plane, vortex ansatz
  E ∝ (x + i·t)·G(x,t), charge sign convention φ = atan2(t, x),
  transverse OAM along y.

## Bliokh & Nori 2012 (ref_bliokh2012)

- K. Y. Bliokh, F. Nori, "Spatiotemporal vortex beams and angular momentum",
  **Phys. Rev. A 86**, 033824 (2012).
- DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevA.86.033824
- Used for: spatiotemporal angular momentum theory. NOTE: OAM *moment*
  normalization for STOV pulses is convention-dependent — in this platform
  it is CANDIDATE_MODEL until a validated source chain is completed.

## Goodman 2017 (ref_goodman2017)

- J. W. Goodman, "Introduction to Fourier Optics", 4th ed., W. H. Freeman
  (2017). ISBN: 978-1-319-11916-4
- Used for: FT sign conventions (§3.1), angular spectrum (§3.10), Fresnel
  propagation (§4.1), Gaussian beams (§3.3).

## Voelz 2011 (ref_voelz2011)

- D. G. Voelz, "Computational Fourier Optics: A MATLAB Tutorial",
  SPIE Press (2011). ISBN: 978-0-8194-8204-4
- Used for: sampling requirements, alias-free propagation bounds,
  computational conventions.

## Andrews & Phillips 2005 (ref_andrews2005)

- L. C. Andrews, R. L. Phillips, "Laser Beam Propagation through Random
  Media", 2nd ed., SPIE Press (2005). ISBN: 978-0-8194-5948-0
- Used for: refractive-index turbulence spectra (von Kármán, Tatarskii).

## Lane et al. 1992 (ref_lane1992)

- R. G. Lane, A. Glindemann, J. C. Dainty, "Simulation of a Kolmogorov
  phase screen", Waves in Random Media 2, 209–224 (1992).
- Used for: FFT phase screen generation method (subharmonics).

## Schmidt 2010 (ref_schmidt2010)

- J. D. Schmidt, "Numerical Simulation of Optical Wave Propagation with
  Examples in MATLAB", SPIE Press (2010).
- Used for: split-step framework and phase screen normalization.
